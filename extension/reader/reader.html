AtomicVaultReader

Choose the folder that holds your Markdown notes. You do this once.

Chrome will ask for permission. On the first grant, Chrome offers a single Allow — accept it. After your next browser restart, the reader shows one Reopen vault button; from that point Chrome can offer "Allow on every visit" — choose it when it appears and reopening becomes automatic.

Nothing leaves your machine. The reader never writes to your files.

Reopen vault

Chrome needs one click to reopen your vault.

Chrome asks this once after a restart. To stop these prompts: Change vault → pick the same folder → choose "Allow on every visit" when Chrome offers it.
